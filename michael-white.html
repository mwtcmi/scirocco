The Connection

Grassroots Motorsports Is
Open Source.

For more than two decades, Mike's professional world has revolved around open source communications technology, first as a user and member of the Asterisk and FreePBX communities, then as an entrepreneur building a business around them. When Sangoma acquired .e4 in 2020, that path brought him inside the company behind the projects. Today, as VP of Open Source, he has a leadership seat at the table for the same communities and technologies he started out using.

Grassroots motorsports works much the same way. Walk through a paddock and people are sharing setups, solving problems, comparing products, helping competitors and learning from what worked on the last run. The strongest recommendations rarely come from an advertisement. They come from another person who has actually used the product.

Build it. Use it. Share what works. Make the whole community better.

Why It Matters To Partners

Influence Starts
In The Paddock.

Mike's professional career has been built around technical communities where credibility comes from participation. His professional network includes nearly 10,000 LinkedIn followers, extending the 799 story beyond the track and into a technology and business audience. The 799 partnership program applies that same philosophy to motorsports. Products should be used, understood and talked about in context , in front of the drivers, builders and enthusiasts who influence what gets bought next.

This isn't about renting space on a race car. It's about giving the right company an authentic place inside the program and the community around it.
