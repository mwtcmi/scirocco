02 · Windows

Lexan
Out.

Every window except the windshield is Lexan. The old panels are scratched and hazy, so I'm replacing all of them with new hard-coated Lexan (Margard MR10), using the old panels as templates. They all come out before the car gets to you.

I'm cleaning the old tape off the openings and priming any bare metal, so they'll be ready for you to paint.

What I'm doing

  • Take out each window (low heat, fishing line through the tape, no prying)
  • Label each panel and bag its screws
  • Clean the openings: eraser wheel, plastic scraper, red Scotch-Brite, wax and grease remover (no hard grinding on the thin flanges)
  • Epoxy primer on any bare metal

For you

  • Paint the window openings
  • If we go with rivnuts for the new windows, set them before paint

03 · Shop List

The
Rest.

Here's the rest of what I'm hoping you can do while the car is with you. We'll walk around it together at drop-off and add anything else.

  • Shock towers: repair (scope to look at together: rust, cracks, reinforcement plates)
  • Buff out or touch up the spot from the last job
  • Weld a tab for the fuel regulator's new spot
  • Paint the radiator shroud
  • Clean and paint the engine bay brackets (coil and alternator brackets)
  • Mask the ground strap's contact point on the body, so no paint goes under it
  • Other touch-ups from the walk-around
Before drop-off I'll disconnect the battery, run the tank low, and protect or pull the fuel lines and regulator near the shock towers for welding.
