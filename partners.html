Motorsport Sponsorship Opportunities

Find Your
Fit.

Racing sponsorship does not have to mean a logo on a fender. Each partnership with 799 is built around what your company needs: season-long exposure, a test platform for racing parts, equipment in the paddock, measurable sales or a race-weekend experience for your customers.

01 / Partnership

Race
Partner

Race car sponsorship

A race partner backs the whole 2027 competition program: nine planned hill climb and time trial weekends across Pennsylvania, Kentucky, Virginia, Michigan and Wisconsin. Instead of one event, your brand travels with a 1976 Volkswagen Scirocco through a full season of grassroots motorsport, from the Pennsylvania Hillclimb Association and Appalachian HillClimb Series to Empire Hill Climb and the Waumandee Time Trials.

What It Can Include

  • Vehicle branding on the 799 Scirocco for the full season or selected events
  • Logo placement on the trailer, paddock setup and race-weekend content
  • Recognition in race reports, results and social media throughout the season
  • Event-by-event support options when a full-season race car sponsorship is not the right fit

02 / Partnership

Technical
Partner

Racing parts & product development

A technical partner puts components or services directly into the development of the car. 799 is never a finished show build: suspension, brakes, power, aero and reliability are tested at real hill climbs and track sprints, and the results are measured on the clock. That makes it a working test platform for racing parts, not a place to bolt something on for a photo. Ground Control, our suspension partner, is an example of how that looks: see the Ground Control build.

What It Can Include

  • Product installed and used in competition, not just displayed
  • Install, setup and development content with real photos and video
  • Honest feedback from hill climb and track sprint use
  • Results and lessons shared with the racing community

03 / Partnership

Paddock
Partner

Paddock & race-weekend equipment

The paddock is where competitors spend their weekend: tools, canopies, generators, jacks, chargers, cameras and everything else a race team needs. A paddock partner puts that equipment into daily use at every 799 event, where other drivers and teams see it working, ask about it and buy the same gear.

What It Can Include

  • Your tools and equipment used in the 799 paddock all season
  • Branded paddock setup where competitors and spectators see it in use
  • Race-weekend content showing products working in the field
  • Real feedback from use in heat, rain and long event days

A commerce partner connects products to sales. Through the 799 Paddock, products we actually use can be recommended through tracked links, discount codes or dealer relationships, so a partnership can be measured in orders, not just impressions.

05 / Partnership

Hospitality
Partner

Race-weekend experiences

A hospitality partner uses a race weekend as a different kind of customer or employee experience. Hill climbs and time trials are open, accessible events where guests can walk the paddock, see the car up close and watch it compete on some of the most established hill climb courses in the eastern United States.

What It Can Include

  • A race-weekend visit for your customers, clients or team
  • Paddock access to see the car and how a race weekend works
  • A memorable alternative to a standard client event or team outing
  • Content and photos from the day to share afterward
