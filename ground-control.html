01 / The Build

MORE THAN
LOWER.

The goal was never just to lower the car. The 799 Scirocco needed suspension that could be adjusted and developed for hill climbs, time trials, and track sprints. Ground Control put together a complete Mk1 VW package, combining front struts, rear coilover shocks, adjustable camber/caster plates, and helper springs.

We went a step further with the finish: custom Kawasaki-green springs to complement the car, black strut housings, and pewter-anodized aluminum components. It's a functional racing setup that looks right at home in the engine bay.

02 / Preparation & Installation

MEASURE.
CUT. FIT.

The hybrid camber/caster plates weren't a simple bolt-on. Installing them required preparing the Mk1 strut towers using Ground Control's Race VW cutting template.

Position the template

Orient the template with its front-of-car and fender references. The drawing shows the passenger side; the driver side is mirrored.

02

Trim for clearance

The dashed boundary identifies the strut-tower material to trim so the plate can sit flat. The template also identifies the four new mounting holes.

03

Fit & align

Install the camber/caster plates and strut assemblies, check clearances and fastener fitment, then move on to alignment and testing.

04 / Ride Height & Stance

THE CAR
ON THE ROAD.

All that work is easier to appreciate when the car is moving. The finished suspension brings the Scirocco into a purposeful, low stance while preserving the narrow factory Mk1 silhouette.

The stance is just one part of the story. Ride height, alignment, damping, and overall balance are still being developed around the demands of real hill-climb courses.

05 / Track Development

THE FIRST TEST
WAS THE RACE.

There wasn't a long test program before the first event. The new suspension's first competitive runs were at Empire Hill Climb, followed by more development time at Grattan Raceway.

SEPTEMBER 2026

Empire Hill Climb

The first real runs on the new suspension happened on the hill. With changing grip, elevation, and an unforgiving point-to-point course, Empire provided an immediate introduction to how the new setup behaved. 799 finished P3, but the setup was still new.

OCTOBER 2026

Grattan Raceway

Grattan's track sprints offered another opportunity to experiment with damping and learn how the Scirocco responds. The work isn't finished. Every outing adds useful information for the next adjustment.

2027 / In the Paddock

BUILT TOGETHER.
DEVELOPED ON THE HILL.

Ground Control Suspension Systems joins the 799 Motorsport paddock for 2027. The collaboration continues beyond installation, through setup, testing, and the process of getting this little green Scirocco dialed in for the next climb.
