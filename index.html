The Car

Built To
Be Driven.

Scirocco 799 is a 1976 Volkswagen Scirocco Type 53 developed for one purpose: competition.

Powered by a naturally aspirated OBD1 ABA 2.0-liter 8-valve breathing through dual 40mm Weber DCOE carburetors, the car combines old-school Volkswagen character with a focused modern competition setup.

The project continues to evolve through real competition in Hill Climb, Time Trials and Track Sprint events. Every change has the same objective: make the car lighter, faster, more predictable and more capable.
