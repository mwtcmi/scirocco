Your Privacy.

What We Collect

When you order, Stripe checkout collects your name, email, phone number, billing address, shipping address if we ship to you, and what you bought. We never see or store your card number: Stripe handles payment.

Back-in-Stock Requests

If you ask us to tell you when something is back, we keep your email address and the item you asked about.

How We Use It

To fill and deliver your order, to contact you about it, to send the back-in-stock email you asked for, and to keep our tax and business records.

Email News

We only send news and offers if you tick the box at checkout. You can stop them any time with the unsubscribe link in the email, or by emailing us. Order and back-in-stock emails are not news and always go to you.

Who Helps Run the Store

Stripe processes payments and sales tax, Resend delivers our emails, and Cloudflare hosts the site and store. They handle your information only to provide those services. We never sell or rent your information.

Website Statistics

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not track you across other websites.

How Long We Keep It

We keep your customer record while you shop with us, and order records for as long as tax and business rules require. Ask us to delete your information and we will, except what we must keep for tax records.

Your Choices

Want to see, correct or delete what we have about you, or stop email news? Email mike@scirocco799.com and we will take care of it.
