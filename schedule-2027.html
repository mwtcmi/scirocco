2027 Planning

Built Around Competition.

This is the planned 2027 program. Final dates and entries will be updated as organizers publish their 2027 calendars and registration opens.

The mix is intentional: 2027 is being built as a hill-climb-first season. The plan connects established Pennsylvania Hillclimb Association events with selected Appalachian HillClimb Series rounds, Empire Hill Climb and a return to the Waumandee Time Trials.

For partners, that means repeated product use, content and paddock exposure across some of the most established grassroots hill climb communities in the eastern United States, rather than a single race weekend.

Organizing a hill climb that would be a good fit for #799? I’m always interested in adding the right events to the calendar. Get in touch.
